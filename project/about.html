About T3 Ward

T3 Ward is a community built on faith, service, and unity. It brings together members of different ages to worship, serve, and grow spiritually under structured leadership.

Ward Unity

The leadership of T3 Ward works together in unity to guide members, support families, and strengthen the spiritual growth of the community. Every organization plays an important role in building a strong and caring ward.

Ward Meeting Schedule

The T3 Ward holds regular meetings to strengthen faith, learning, and unity among members. Everyone is encouraged to attend and participate.

  • Sunday School: 08:00 AM
  • Sacrament Meeting: 09:10 AM

These meetings are important for spiritual growth, worship, and community support.
