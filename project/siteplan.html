T3 Ward Website Plan

The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints

Site Name

T3 Ward LDS Community

This name identifies the T3 Ward of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and represents a community-centered website that provides information, inspiration, and communication resources for members and visitors.

Site Purpose

The website will provide information about the T3 Ward, including meeting schedules, announcements, leadership information, youth programs, service projects, inspirational messages, contact information, and photographs. The site will be responsive and accessible on both desktop and mobile devices.

Scenarios

  • What time are the Sunday worship services held?
  • How can I contact the ward leadership?
  • What activities are available for youth members?
  • Where can I find information about upcoming service projects?

Color Schema

Navy Blue (#003366)

Used for headings, navigation menus, and important content areas.

Gold (#D4AF37)

Used for accents, buttons, borders, and highlighted sections.

White (#FFFFFF)

Used as the primary background color for readability.

Typography

Merriweather

Used for headings and section titles.

Roboto

Used for body text, paragraphs, and lists.
